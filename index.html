Our story

From scattered notes to a real study system.

Farm4Glass started with a simple frustration: DECA preparation felt scattered, overwhelming, and inconsistent. As competitors ourselves, we wanted a better way to prepare for exams, roleplays, and written events. All three of us also love graphic design — a hard skill to build — so we made Canva and Adobe courses specifically for DECA.

We built Farm4Glass to bring it all together: structured courses, realistic practice, gamified progression, and resources created by students who have actually competed. We've also invited guest speakers — previous ICDC winners and finalists — to share what actually worked for them.

Our mission is simple: help every DECA student walk into competition day confident and prepared.
